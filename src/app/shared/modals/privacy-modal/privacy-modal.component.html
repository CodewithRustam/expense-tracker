Privacy Policy & Security

Encrypted & Compliant

Protected by TLS 1.3 encryption and secure session tokens in accordance with GDPR & CCPA frameworks.

Core Security Standards

End-to-End Transport Security

All data exchanged between your browser and SplitX servers is encrypted using standard 256-bit TLS/SSL protocols.

Salted Password & Token Vault

Passwords are never stored in plain text. Authentication relies on cryptographically signed JWT tokens with automatic expiration.

Strict Group Data Isolation

Expenses and settlements are partitioned strictly to authorized group participants. Uninvited users cannot access or view your ledgers.

Collected Data & Purpose
ACCOUNT INFO

Name & Email Address

Used strictly to identify you in shared groups, deliver notifications, and secure password recovery.

FINANCIAL LEDGERS

Expenses, Splits & Payments

Stored to compute group balances, generate trend charts, and maintain accurate settlement history.

SECURITY LOGS

Device Tokens

Used to detect suspicious login attempts, prevent session hijacking, and safeguard your account.
